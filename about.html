ABOUT SPLITLY!

No drama.
No complicated math.

Splitly! is a simple bill-splitting tool made to make sharing bills with friends easier and less awkward.

Whether you're splitting everything equally, paying based on what you ordered, or setting a custom amount, Splitly! does the math for you.

Just add your people, choose how you want to split the bill, and let Splitly! handle the rest.
